🌸 Waste Awareness Garden

Small habits grow into a cleaner community.

🟢

Wet Waste

Put food waste, vegetable peels and other organic waste into the green bin.

🔵

Dry Waste

Keep paper, cardboard, plastic bottles and recyclable materials separately.

🟤

Composting

Turn kitchen and garden waste into useful compost instead of sending it to landfill.

♻️

Recycle

Reuse materials whenever possible and send recyclable waste to proper recycling facilities.

🌱

Reduce Plastic

Use reusable bags, bottles and containers instead of single-use plastic.

🌍

Keep Earth Clean

Never throw garbage on roads, drains, parks or public places.
